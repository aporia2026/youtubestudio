# 2026-05-22 — LTX-2.3 spike verdict

**Status:** Spiked. Downloads complete + workflow built but the model fails to initialize under vanilla ComfyUI. Deferred until upstream tooling catches up (or a deeper integration session is dedicated).

## Goal

Determine whether LTX-2.3 22B (Q3_K_M GGUF) is usable as a third local i2v option alongside Wan 2.2 and HunyuanVideo. LTX-2.3 promises 10-second clips at 1280×896 in 10–15 min on 16 GB per third-party reports — a meaningfully different sweet spot from Wan/Hunyuan's 2-3s clips at smaller canvases.

## What works

✅ **Downloads** (14.6 GB total, all under `D:\AI\ComfyUI\ComfyUI\models\`):
- `unet/ltx-2.3-22b-dev-Q3_K_M.gguf` — 10.8 GB
- `text_encoders/ltx-2.3-22b-dev_embeddings_connectors.safetensors` — 2.31 GB
- `vae/ltx-2.3-22b-dev_video_vae.safetensors` — 1.45 GB

✅ **Workflow scaffold** at `src/lib/comfyui/workflows/ltx-2.3-i2v.json` — 14 nodes, mirrors the topology of ComfyUI's published LTX-Video 0.9.5 image-to-video example: `UnetLoaderGGUF` → `CLIPLoader(type=ltxv)` → `VAELoader` → `CLIPTextEncode×2` → `LoadImage` → `LTXVPreprocess` → `LTXVImgToVideo` → `LTXVConditioning` → `LTXVScheduler` + `KSamplerSelect` → `SamplerCustom` → `VAEDecode` → `SaveWEBM`. All required + optional inputs validated against the live `/object_info` schemas.

✅ **ComfyUI sees the files** — both `UnetLoaderGGUF` (`ltx-2.3-22b-dev-Q3_K_M.gguf`) and `CLIPLoader` (`ltx-2.3-22b-dev_embeddings_connectors.safetensors` with `type: 'ltxv'`) accept the names without complaint at submission time.

## What doesn't work

❌ **Runtime: "Cannot copy out of meta tensor; no data!"** at node 5 (`CLIPTextEncode` of the negative prompt). The embeddings_connectors model loads structurally but its weights stay on the `meta` device — the standard `CLIPLoader` path doesn't materialise the tensors onto GPU before the forward pass.

Smoke command: `python hiccup-analysis/<ad-hoc inline>` submitting the workflow at 768×512 × 25 frames × 25 steps. Submission succeeded; KSampler never ran; failure surfaces on first text-encode forward.

## Why this happens (hypothesis)

LTX-Video 2.3 22B uses a different text-encoder architecture than the older 2B model (per the [WaveSpeed write-up](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/ltx-2-3-comfyui-setup-two-stage-pipeline/), 2.3 uses a Gemma-based encoder; 0.9.5 used T5XXL). Vanilla ComfyUI's `CLIPLoader` knows the `ltxv` type from the old model and likely loads the embeddings file with the wrong dispatch, leaving the actual Gemma weights as meta tensors.

The unsloth + Patreon "16 GB installer" posts mention they ship a **custom workflow JSON** alongside the GGUFs that handles this. The vanilla example workflow at `comfyanonymous.github.io/ComfyUI_examples/ltxv/` only covers the older 2B model.

## Decision

**Defer** rather than burn another half-day on custom loader work. Two viable resumption paths:

1. **Wait for upstream**: city96 / unsloth / ComfyUI-Org will likely publish either a working node update or a verified workflow JSON for 2.3 22B GGUF on 16 GB cards. Subscribe to the unsloth model card discussions and pick this back up when one lands.

2. **Pull the unsloth installer workflow** — the [Patreon post](https://www.patreon.com/posts/ltx-2-3-22b-gguf-152753064) bundles a known-working workflow specifically for the 16 GB case. Costs a Patreon subscription; outputs a workflow JSON we can adapt + check in. Best path when budget allows.

## Why deferral is the right call right now

- Wan 2.2 + HunyuanVideo (local) + cloud Kie clip models cover the user's existing clip needs.
- LTX-2.3 would unlock longer clips (10s vs 2-3s) at higher canvas (1280×896 vs 704×416 / 480×272) but at proportionally slower wall-clock (10-15 min vs 3-5 min).
- For a long-form YouTube video at typical 12-20 cuts, LTX-2.3 would mean 2-5 hours of render time vs Wan/Hunyuan's 30-50 min. Not obviously better for batch production.
- The failure is a tooling problem upstream, not a hardware or licensing problem. Sitting and waiting two weeks for upstream to resolve is much cheaper than reverse-engineering the right loader chain ourselves.
